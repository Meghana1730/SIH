## How to read this document

### Classification labels
| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `REAL_PUBLIC` | Real data that can be downloaded or read directly from a public source |
| `REAL_MANUAL` | Real data that a person must collect by hand: copying from web pages, reading PDFs, surveys, interviews |
| `SYNTHETIC` | Generated by our own rules; `is_synthetic = true` |
| `TODO_VERIFY` | Might exist or be usable, but key facts (access, fields, license) are unconfirmed |

### Verification status
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `AUTO_CHECKED` | On **2026-09-29** an automated web check (search + page fetch) found this URL on the organisation's site, and some or all of its contents were seen. **Descriptions of contents are summaries and may be imprecise.** A team member must open the page, confirm it, and change the status to `TEAM_VERIFIED`. |
| `SEEN_IN_SEARCH` | The URL appeared in search results, but the page could not be opened automatically (blocked, JavaScript-only or timed out) |
| `UNVERIFIED` | Nothing has been checked yet |
| `TEAM_VERIFIED` | A team member confirmed it by hand. None yet. |
| `REJECTED` | Not usable for us |

### Rules this document follows
- **Only URLs that the automated check actually saw or opened are listed.** No URL here was typed from memory. If a URL is not known, the entry says `TODO-VERIFY`.
- **"Free"** means free to access. It does *not* mean free to reuse. Always read the license column.
- **Licenses:** for most Indian government portals **no license or terms of use text was found** by the automated check. Treat these as "public information, attribution required, terms `TODO-VERIFY`". The **Government Open Data License – India (GODL)** applies only to datasets published on data.gov.in.
- **Scraping:** no official statement allowing automated scraping was found for any portal. Our default is **download published files, use official APIs, or copy small samples by hand. No scrapers.**
