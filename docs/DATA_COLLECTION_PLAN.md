This plan explains **who collects what, when, how, and how we know it's done**. Source IDs such as `S18`, `P01`, `C01` and `Y06` refer to entries in the inventory.

---

## 1. Principles (read first)

1. **Real where possible, synthetic where necessary, and always labelled.** Every record carries `is_synthetic = true/false`. No exceptions.
2. **No invented facts.** No guessed government codes, statistics or URLs. If you are unsure, write `TODO-VERIFY`.
3. **Keep originals.** Every downloaded file goes into `data/raw/<source_id>/` untouched, with a small note: where it came from, the date, and the license.
4. **Provenance on every row** (see [Section 6](#6-file-and-provenance-conventions)).
5. **Legal and ethical collection.**
   - No scraping against a website's terms of use.
   - No personal data unless we have consent for it.
   - Attribute sources as their licenses require.
6. **Enough, not everything.** We need a small, clean, well-documented dataset for 1 sector and 4 districts, not all of India.

## 2. Labels we use

### Classification: where the data comes from
| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `REAL_PUBLIC` | Real data from a public source, which we can download or read directly |
| `REAL_MANUAL` | Real data that a person must collect or transcribe by hand: reading PDFs, copying from search-only web pages, surveys, interviews, curation |
| `SYNTHETIC` | Generated by our own rules. Must have `is_synthetic = true`. |
| `TODO_VERIFY` | We *think* it exists or is usable, but nobody has confirmed it yet |

### Verification status: how sure we are
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `UNVERIFIED` | Nobody has checked it yet |
| `AUTO_CHECKED` | An automated web check found the URL on an official domain, but no team member has confirmed the contents |
| `TEAM_VERIFIED` | A team member opened it, confirmed the contents and license, and recorded the date |
| `REJECTED` | Checked and found unusable (not available, wrong granularity, license problem) |

> Anything we show in the pitch must be `TEAM_VERIFIED` or clearly labelled synthetic or illustrative.

## 5. Collection tasks by data need

Each task lists its **sources**, **classification**, **method**, **output file**, and **done when**.

### 5.1 Job postings (need 01)
- **Sources:** S05 (public datasets: check the license), S01 NCS, S03 Mahaswayam, S04 Apprenticeship portal (read manually), Y01 (synthetic).
- **Method:**
  1. Download public datasets that the license allows us to use.
  2. Filter for electrical, EV and solar keywords.
  3. Keep title, description, location, date, salary and experience. **Drop phone numbers and emails.**
  4. From official portals, only copy a small manual sample (e.g. 30–50 postings) *if their terms allow*. Record each posting's page and date.
- **Expect:** real public datasets may be old, IT-heavy, or have few postings in our 4 districts *(TODO-VERIFY after inspection)*. Plan: use **real postings to build and test skill extraction**, and **synthetic postings (Y01)** for the district time series with planted patterns.
- **Output:** `data/curated/job_postings_real.csv`, `data/synthetic/job_postings_synthetic.csv`
- **Done when:** ≥ 500 real sector-relevant postings (any location) for NLP testing, and ≥ 2,000 synthetic postings across 4 districts × 6 quarters.

### 5.2 Employer demand (need 02)
- **Sources:** P01 (our own survey), Y02 (synthetic seed).
- **Method:** see [Section 7](#7-primary-research-protocols-surveys-and-consultations).
- **Output:** `data/curated/employer_survey_responses_real.csv` (anonymised) and `data/synthetic/employer_survey_responses_synthetic.csv`
- **Done when:** ≥ 10 real responses (the target is 20), plus 60–100 synthetic ones.

### 5.3 Industry consultations (need 03)
- **Sources:** P02 (our interviews). Fallback: Y03, clearly labelled role-play.
- **Output:** `data/curated/consultations/<id>.md`, one file per interview with anonymised notes and approved quotes.
- **Done when:** 3–5 consultations, each with at least 3 skills mentioned and 1 approved quote.

### 5.4 Sector growth (need 04)
- **Sources:** S09 Vahan (EV registrations by RTO; plan for manual transcription), S08 Udyam (MSMEs by district with LGD code, via data.gov.in dataset D7), S10 MNRE / PM Surya Ghar (rooftop solar, **state level only**), S11 BEE (charging stations, TODO-VERIFY), S07 PLFS (**state-level context only**), S28 Census 2011 (population, for per-capita normalisation).
- **Method:** record **only what is officially shown**, at the finest level available (state, district or RTO). Note the level honestly. Do **not** turn state figures into district figures without labelling it as an estimate.
- **Output:** `data/curated/sector_indicators.csv` (columns: `indicator`, `sector`, `geography_level`, `geography_name`, `district_lgd` (if applicable), `period`, `value`, `unit`, plus provenance).
- **Done when:** at least 1 real indicator for EV and 1 for solar, at the finest available level, is `TEAM_VERIFIED`.

### 5.5 Industry events (need 05)
- **Sources:** S14 PIB, S13 state industries department, S12 MIDC, reputable news (only as a pointer to an official source), Y04 (the hypothetical demo event).
- **Method:** list real announcements relevant to EV, solar or electrical in or near our districts, with a citation for each. Record the stated jobs **only if officially stated**. The demo's "EV plant announced in Nashik" is **hypothetical** and must be labelled synthetic.
- **Output:** `data/curated/sector_events.csv`
- **Done when:** 5–15 real events with citations, plus 1–3 synthetic demo events flagged `is_synthetic = true`.

### 5.6 Skills (need 06)
- **Sources:** NOS performance criteria (S15/S17), DGT syllabi (S18), real postings (S05). Optional enrichment from S30 ESCO, S31 O*NET, S32 Lightcast (check licenses; use them only to find alias ideas, never as the backbone).
- **Output:** `data/curated/skills.csv`, `data/curated/skill_aliases.csv`
- **Done when:** 150–300 skills for the sector, each with a type, a description and a source reference. At least 2 aliases per skill, including Hindi and Marathi for the top 50.

### 5.7 Job roles (need 07)
- **Sources:** S15 NQR (QP job roles), S02 NCO (occupation codes), S17 SSCs.
- **Output:** `data/curated/job_roles.csv`, `data/curated/role_skill.csv`
- **Done when:** 15–25 roles, each linked to a QP and/or an NCO code where officially found (`TODO-VERIFY` otherwise), with 5–15 skills per role.

### 5.8 ITI and course information (need 08)

## 6. File and provenance conventions

### Folders
```
data/
  raw/<source_id>/        original downloads, untouched, plus a SOURCE_NOTE.md (URL, date, license)
  curated/                real data, cleaned into CSVs (REAL_PUBLIC / REAL_MANUAL)
  synthetic/              generated data (every row is_synthetic = true)
  gold/                   hand-labelled evaluation data
  geo/                    map files
  research/               source_inventory.csv (the working tracker)
```

### Provenance columns: required in every CSV we create
| Column | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `source_id` | `S18` | From the inventory |
| `source_ref` | "Electrician syllabus PDF, page 12" | Where exactly in the source |
| `fetched_at` | `2026-10-02` | Format YYYY-MM-DD |
| `collected_by` | `R2` | Owner code, not a personal name |
| `license_note` | "GODL-India, attribution required" or `TODO-VERIFY` | |
| `is_synthetic` | `false` | `true` for anything generated |
| `verification_status` | `TEAM_VERIFIED` | See Section 2 |

### Formatting rules
- File and column names use `lower_snake_case`.
- **UTF-8 encoding**, which is essential for Hindi and Marathi. In Excel on Windows, use **Save As → "CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited)"**. A normal "CSV" save will corrupt Devanagari text.
- Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`; quarters are `2026Q3`.
- Districts are always referenced by their **LGD code** once it has been verified, never by name alone.
- One row per record. Multiple values in one cell are separated by `;`.

## 7. Primary research protocols (surveys and consultations)

### 7.1 Consent script (read or send before every survey or interview)
> "We are students building a project for the Smart India Hackathon, about matching skill training with local job needs. Your answers will be used only for this student project and shown only as combined summaries. We will not show your name or your business name unless you allow it. You can skip any question or stop at any time. Is that okay?"

Record `consent = yes/no`. **No consent means no record.**

### 7.2 Employer survey (under 2 minutes; English, Hindi, Marathi)
1. Business type: electrical contractor / solar installer / EV dealer or service / manufacturer / other
2. Size: micro / small / medium / large (let them self-report)
3. District
4. Which roles do you expect to hire for in the next 12 months? How many for each (approximately)?
5. Which skills are hardest to find in new hires?
6. What level do you need: helper / technician / senior technician / supervisor?
7. Which topics do freshers from ITIs or short courses lack?
8. Would you offer apprenticeships or hire trainees if courses taught these skills? (yes / maybe / no; how many)
9. May we contact you again to review proposed course changes? (yes / no)

**Do not record:** personal phone numbers or names in the data CSV. Keep the contact list **separate**, in a private file that is not committed to git, and delete it after the hackathon.

### 7.3 Outreach list (who to ask)
- The team's personal and family networks in the four districts (electricians, solar installers, EV showrooms and service centres)
- Local businesses found through normal manual searching. Call or visit; **do not scrape** listing sites.
- College and ITI placement cells and alumni, if reachable
- Industry associations, through their public contact details

### 7.4 Consultation interview guide (15–20 minutes)
1. What does a new technician do in their first 3 months at your business?
2. Which tasks do freshers struggle with most?
3. What has changed in your work in the last 2–3 years (new equipment, EV, solar, smart meters, safety rules)?
4. Which topics taught in training are rarely used in your work now?
5. What equipment should trainees practise on before joining?
6. What would make you trust a training institute's certificate more?
7. Would you review a proposed new module if we sent one?

**Recording rule:** take written notes. Record audio only with explicit consent. **Paraphrase** in the notes, and use a **direct quote** only if the person approves it. Save as `consultations/C-<number>.md` with no personal names.

## 8. Synthetic data rules and planted patterns

R6 writes the full `docs/SYNTHETIC_DATA_SPEC.md` using these rules.

### 8.1 Rules
1. `is_synthetic = true` on every generated row, and a visible "synthetic" badge in the UI.
2. **Deterministic:** the same seed always produces the same data.
3. Value ranges (salaries, seats, placement rates) are **team assumptions, documented as assumptions**, not presented as real statistics.
4. Synthetic outcomes are attached to **fictional demo institutes only**.
5. Every planted pattern is written down *before* generation, so we can test that the system rediscovers it.

### 8.2 Gold-set labelling guideline (for C07)
- For each posting, list every skill (use names from `skills.csv`; if none fits, propose a new one), its band (1–4), and the role.
- **Not skills:** job conditions ("two-wheeler preferred"), benefits, and personal traits unless teachable ("good communication" counts as a core skill).
- Each posting is labelled by one person and checked by a second. Resolve disagreements together.

### 8.3 Planted patterns (synthetic; not claims about reality)
| ID | Pattern the generator must create | What the system should detect |
|---|---|---|
| PP-1 | EV-charger installation and solar PV installation mentions rise steadily over 4–6 quarters in Nashik and Pune | Skills flagged EMERGING; demand rising |
| PP-2 | The Electrician course at "Example ITI A, Nashik" covers almost none of those skills | Low coverage; OUTDATED flag; ADD-module recommendation |
| PP-3 | A wiring-helper-type course in Kolhapur has many more graduates than openings, and low placement | OVERSUPPLIED flag; REDUCE_SEATS recommendation |
| PP-4 | A legacy skill (e.g. manual motor rewinding) declines in mentions over 4 quarters | DECLINING skill; DEMOTE-module candidate |
| PP-5 | A hypothetical "EV component plant announced in Nashik" event | Forecast uplift 3–6 quarters later |
| PP-6 | Institutes that added a relevant module show better placement in later cohorts | The feedback loop increases the weight of the relevant signals |

## 10. Risks and fallbacks

| Risk | Fallback |
|---|---|
| A government website is down or blocks access on demo day | We never depend on live access. All sources are downloaded during Week 1 and stored in `data/raw/`. |
| No district-level figure exists (only state-level) | Use the state figure labelled "state-level context". Any district split is labelled an **estimate** or is synthetic. |
| Public job datasets have few relevant postings | Use them for NLP testing; use synthetic postings for the district time series (PP-1). |
| Few employers respond to the survey | Use real responses as "illustrative voices" and synthetic responses for volume, clearly labelled. |
| The boundary map license is unclear | Use a district-point map, or a simple schematic map. |
| QP or syllabus PDFs are hard to parse | Transcribe manually. The MVP needs only 4–6 courses. |
| Unclear license on a source | Don't use it in the demo until the license is clear; mark it `REJECTED` or `TODO_VERIFY`. |
